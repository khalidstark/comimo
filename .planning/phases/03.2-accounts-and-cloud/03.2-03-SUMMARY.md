# Phase 03.2 Plan 03: About You Summary

**Right after sign-up, Comimo now asks who you are (a selfie or photo, what you're into, how you are with people, what people call you) and turns the account's narrator into you: your face drawn from the photo, your name and nicknames, and a personality the script model writes your lines from.**

## Performance

- **Tasks:** 2 of 2 auto tasks (Task 3 is the human checkpoint)
- **Commits:** see below; nothing pushed
- **Files:** 7 created, 13 modified

## Accomplishments

- **/welcome (About you)**, four steps in the sign-up page's zine style, phone-first: progress dots, Back, Next, "Skip this step" on every step, "Do this later" at the top.
  1. Photo: "Take a selfie" (`capture="user"`) or "Choose a photo", a consent tick, Zine/Painterly choice. The photo is shrunk on the device, sent once to find and describe the person and once to draw the sheet, then dropped. The cut-out shows while drawing, the user can keep answering in the meantime, and the art panel turns into their sheet when done.
  2. Interests: 16 chips plus up to 3 typed ones.
  3. How you are with people: 12 chips with one-line hints; Next needs at least one (skipping is fine).
  4. Name (prefilled from the account), nicknames and Arabic spellings, optional pronouns.
- **Self character**: `applyAboutYou` keeps the id, sets name, aliases, roleLabel "You", look and sheet from the photo, `personality` from the chips (deterministic, at most 300 characters) and a sample line from their first manner; `about` keeps the raw choices. Saved through the store, so it syncs like any cast edit.
- **Script**: the speaker's brief carries `about`; the route keeps only known chips, clean typed interests and pronouns; the prompt adds `withPeople`, `interests`, `pronouns` for the self member and a rule to write their dialogue and narration in that voice without inventing anything.
- **Cast page**: the "You" card shows the chips, "Edit about you" (reopens /welcome) and, while on the stand-in look, "Add my photo".
- **/record**: accounts with no About-you answers get a one-time card ("Not now" sets the synced `aboutYou:asked`); accounts on the stand-in look get a small "Add your photo" line.
